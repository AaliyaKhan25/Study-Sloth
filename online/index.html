Turn your notes into a study set.

Upload a PDF or paste text. Get a summary, notes by topic with page references, flashcards, a quiz and viva questions. Nothing to install.

Your text is sent to an open-weight model hosted by Ollama Cloud to write the notes. Nothing is saved on this site and results disappear after an hour. Text-based PDFs only, first 30 pages. Lecture recordings, board photos and lab records need the local version: GitHub.
